# Improved Onion Varieties Released (ICAR-DOGR)

Source: Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR), "Improved Onion Varieties Released", source credited on the page as DOGR, Rajgurunagar (ICAR-Directorate of Onion and Garlic Research). URL: https://icar.org.in/en/node/2087 (accessed 30 Oct 2026). The page carries no publication date. It reports five varieties recommended for release at national level at a workshop of the All India Network Research Project on Onion and Garlic (AINRPOG).

How this file was prepared: text is reproduced from the page, split so each paragraph names its variety. Abbreviation: DAT = days after transplanting. Sugar-content (TSS) figures printed on the page were left out because they were garbled in the page text.

ICAR-DOGR onion variety Bhima Super: A red onion variety identified for release for kharif season in Chhattisgarh, Delhi, Gujarat, Haryana, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha, Punjab, Rajasthan and Tamil Nadu. It can also be grown in late kharif. It is reported to have an average yield of 20-22 t/ha in kharif and 40-45 t/ha in late kharif. Bulbs attain maturity within 100-105 DAT in kharif and 110-120 DAT in late kharif. It produces mostly single centered bulbs.

ICAR-DOGR onion variety Bhima Dark Red: Identified for release for kharif season in Chhattisgarh, Delhi, Gujarat, Haryana, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha, Punjab, Rajasthan and Tamil Nadu. Average marketable yield is 20-22 t/ha. It is recommended particularly due to its attractive dark red flat globe bulbs. It attains maturity within 95-100 DAT.

ICAR-DOGR onion variety Bhima Red: Already recommended for rabi season in Maharashtra and Madhya Pradesh, and also recommended for release for kharif season in Delhi, Gujarat, Haryana, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Punjab, Rajasthan and Tamil Nadu. It can also be grown in late kharif. Maturity is 105-110 DAT during kharif and 110-120 DAT during late kharif and rabi seasons. The average marketable yield is 19-21 t/ha in kharif, 48-52 t/ha in late kharif and 30-32 t/ha in rabi season. It can be stored up to 3 months in rabi.

ICAR-DOGR onion variety Bhima Shweta: A white onion variety already recommended for rabi, also recommended for release for kharif in Chhattisgarh, Gujarat, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha, Rajasthan and Tamil Nadu. It matures within 110-120 DAT. It has medium keeping quality and can be stored up to 3 months. Average marketable yield is 18-20 t/ha in kharif and 26-30 t/ha in rabi.

ICAR-DOGR onion variety Bhima Shubhra: A white onion variety recommended for kharif season in Chhattisgarh, Gujarat, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha, Rajasthan and Tamil Nadu. It is also recommended for late kharif in Maharashtra. It matures in 110-115 DAT during kharif and 120-130 DAT in late kharif. It is a medium storer with a capacity to tolerate environmental fluctuation. Average marketable yield is 18-20 t/ha during kharif and 36-42 t/ha during late kharif.
