# Onion Cold Storage - Potential to Stabilize Price Fluctuation (ICAR-DOGR)

Source: Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR), "Onion Cold Storage - Potential to Stabilize Price Fluctuation". Technology of ICAR-Directorate on Onion and Garlic Research (DOGR), Pune. URL: https://icar.org.in/en/node/4701 (accessed 30 Oct 2026). The page carries no publication date.

How this file was prepared: sentences and table values are reproduced from the page; tables were rewritten as sentences with the same numbers. Each paragraph starts with a context label so it can be understood on its own.

ICAR-DOGR onion storage, why it matters: Onion is mainly cultivated in three seasons i.e. during kharif, late kharif, and rabi. The crop harvested during rabi accounts for 60% of onion production and hits the markets from March to June. The same crop continues to meet the consumer demand till October-November every year before the kharif crop is harvested and brought to the market. Therefore, it is pivotal to successfully store rabi onion so as to maintain its supply in the markets.

ICAR-DOGR onion storage, losses and prices: Onion is a semi-perishable crop and 30-40% of the crop gets lost during storage. During natural calamities, the losses go beyond 40%. The supply volatility creates market distress causing a steep rise in the price of onion that eventually affects the end consumers. An adequate storage facility will help in checking the supply volatility and steep rise in onion prices.

ICAR-DOGR onion storage, ventilated structures: In India, onions are mostly stored in ventilated storage structures without any control of temperature and relative humidity. A low cost thatched roof bamboo storage structure is low cost and easy to construct, but leads to up to 42% losses of onion during four months of storage. A bottom and side ventilated storage structure results in losses up to 46% in four months of storage.

ICAR-DOGR onion storage, cold storage: In cold storage, onions are stored at 0-5 degrees C and 60-65% relative humidity, which leads to much lesser losses than ventilated storage. The cost of construction and running cost are very high as the energy required is high. Other problems are condensation, and the need for a lot of energy and time. The bulbs start sprouting immediately after they are removed from the cold storage.

ICAR-DOGR onion storage, the DOGR-cold storage design: The structure developed by ICAR-DOGR, Pune, stores onion bulbs in controlled conditions with proper ventilation, using low energy while minimizing sprouting, rotting, and physiological weight loss. It was designed under Public Private Partnership mode, to maintain a temperature of 27 +/- 2 degrees C and relative humidity of 60 to 65% with an air circulation system. The concept has been filed for patent (application number 201821049581).

ICAR-DOGR onion storage, total loss after 120 days (4 months): DOGR-cold storage (27 +/- 2 degrees C) 7.14%; cold storage (0 degrees C) 5.00%; ventilated storage 54.89%; bulk storage at Rajgurunagar 54.64%; bulk storage at Khalus 59.04%.

ICAR-DOGR onion storage, sprouting after taking bulbs out of storage: Cold storage (0 degrees C) showed 27%, 29% and 34% sprouting at 15, 30 and 60 days. DOGR-cold storage showed 0%, 0% and 9.12% at 15, 30 and 60 days. Black mold in cold storage (0 degrees C) was 0.8%, 1.5% and 15.2% at 15, 30 and 60 days; the page gives no black mold figures for DOGR-cold storage.

ICAR-DOGR onion storage, advantages stated for DOGR-cold storage: Running cost and construction cost are expected to be less than cold storage. It can be built for capacity as low as 2 tonnes and scaled up to 2000 tonnes. It can also be used for curing of onion during kharif and rabi season. It will be useful to store kharif onion with negligible sprouting, which is a major constraint for storage of kharif onion. Farmers can build low-capacity units in their field to avoid distress selling during the main season (May-June), especially in Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Delhi and Bihar, where temperature reaches up to 46 degrees C and storage of onion is extremely difficult.
