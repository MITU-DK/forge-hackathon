# ICAR Kharif Agro-Advisories for Farmers 2025 - Khandesh / Northern Maharashtra (includes Nashik)

Source: ICAR Kharif Agro-Advisories for Farmers 2025, Indian Council of Agricultural Research, New Delhi, May 2025, Maharashtra chapter, section "Khandesh / Northern Maharashtra", pages 157-159. How this file was prepared: sentences are reproduced from the publication; PDF ligature errors were repaired. Sentences that name pesticide or herbicide products or give their doses were left out on purpose (project safety rule: the farmer is told to follow the product label and the local agriculture officer). Numbers that are unclear in the printed original were also left out. This is the Kharif 2025 advisory, so check the season before relying on dates.

ICAR Kharif Agro-Advisory 2025, Khandesh / Northern Maharashtra, region: This region of Maharashtra is comprised of Jalgaon, Dhule, Nandurbar, Nashik. Rainfall of the region is 600-900 mm and it has medium to deep black soils. The state agricultural university is Mahatma Phule Krishi Vidyapeeth, Rahuri, Ahmednagar.

ICAR Kharif Agro-Advisory 2025, Nashik region, Onion (Kharif), nursery: For effective nursery preparation, raise beds enriched with FYM, copper oxychloride, and a balanced mixed fertilizer. Seed treatment against fungal infection and weed control in the nursery are advised in the publication; product names and doses are left out here, so ask the local agriculture officer.

ICAR Kharif Agro-Advisory 2025, Nashik region, Onion (Kharif), fertilizer and protection: At planting, use a fertilizer dose of 100:50:50 NPK. The remaining nitrogen is applied at 45 days after transplanting (DAT). To manage fungal diseases, integrate Trichoderma along with regular fungicide sprays every 10-15 days. For thrips control, install sticky traps and use insecticides as required based on pest observation; follow the product label and the local agriculture officer for which product and how much.

ICAR Kharif Agro-Advisory 2025, Nashik region, Soybean: The crop must be sown before 1 week of July, after good rain. Give a hoeing at 15-20 DAS. Apply water at branching (30-35 DAS) and flowering (40-45 DAS).

ICAR Kharif Agro-Advisory 2025, Nashik region, Cotton, land and sowing: Use medium to deep black cotton soils with good drainage for optimal crop performance. Prepare land with 1-2 deep ploughings followed by harrowing; adopt ridge-furrow or broad bed-furrow method. Sow Bt cotton hybrids like RCH-2 BG II or NCS-855 BG II from mid-June to early July, depending on monsoon onset. Maintain spacing of 120 x 60 cm for hybrids and 90 x 45 cm for varieties; use seed rate of 450-500 g/acre.

ICAR Kharif Agro-Advisory 2025, Nashik region, Cotton, nutrition and water: Apply basal dose of NPK @ 50:25:25 kg/ha based on soil test; supplement with FYM @ 5-10 t/ha. Apply zinc sulphate @ 25 kg/ha in zinc-deficient soils. Ensure field has proper drainage to prevent waterlogging, especially during early growth stages. Provide protective irrigation during long dry spells, particularly during flowering and boll development. Apply top dressing of nitrogen at 30 and 60 DAS for vigorous growth.

ICAR Kharif Agro-Advisory 2025, Nashik region, Cotton, pests and harvest: Install 5 pheromone traps per hectare to monitor bollworms. Use plant growth regulators like NAA to minimize flower and boll drop, if observed. Harvest mature, dry bolls; avoid harvesting during or immediately after rain to prevent boll rotting.

ICAR Kharif Agro-Advisory 2025, Nashik region, Pigeon pea: Recommended varieties for sowing include Phule Trupti, BDN-711, and Renuka, with planting done before the first week of July. Suggested spacing is 60 x 20 cm, 90 x 20 cm, or 180 x 30 cm, depending on field conditions. Apply fertilizers at a rate of 25:50 kg N:P per hectare. The seed rate varies, 12-15 kg/ha for normal spacing and 5-6 kg/ha for wider spacing. Intercropping is recommended with bajra, soybean, sorghum, cotton, moong, or urad for better land utilization. Irrigate at 30-35 days after sowing (DAS), 60-70 DAS, and during the pod-filling stage. Perform one hoeing at 15-20 DAS followed by hand weeding for effective weed control.

ICAR Kharif Agro-Advisory 2025, Nashik region, Black gram: The recommended seed rate is 15-20 kg/ha with a spacing of 30 x 10 cm. Apply fertilizers at a rate of 20:40 kg N:P per hectare. Intercropping with pigeon pea and other kharif crops is advised for better resource utilization. Irrigation should be provided at critical stages such as flowering and pod filling to ensure healthy growth and yield.

ICAR Kharif Agro-Advisory 2025, Nashik region, Pomegranate (Mrig Bahar): Pomegranate cultivation during Mrig Bahar requires pruning to remove diseased branches. Pre-flowering fertilization includes 700 g urea, 1600 g SSP, and 400 g MOP per tree. At the fruiting stage, apply 500 g DAP + 100 g MOP per tree at the lemon size stage, and 200 g 19:19:19 + 100 g MOP at the guava size stage.

ICAR Kharif Agro-Advisory 2025, Nashik region, Banana: Banana is planted on raised beds with a paired row system, ideally after 2-3 monsoon showers. Varieties like Grand 9 and Basarai are recommended. Green manuring and proper drainage are crucial during heavy rains. Practices like de-navelling during fruiting, and irrigation at fruit development, are recommended for healthy crop growth. During flowering/fruiting stage, carry out de-navelling (removal of male inflorescences for nutrient diversion) for better growth of banana fruits. Apply irrigation at fruit development stage.

ICAR Kharif Agro-Advisory 2025, Nashik region, Grape: the publication gives chemical-based pruning and pest steps for grape; they are left out here because they are pesticide and plant-growth-chemical doses. Ask the local agriculture officer or the grape research institute.
